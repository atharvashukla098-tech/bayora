What Bayora does not guarantee

Honest limits

  • The mock target proves controls fire on defined scenarios — not real-model behavior.
  • Docker-gated runtime controls report SKIPPED when Docker is absent. Never PASS.
  • A signature proves the report matches its key — not the truth of every event.
  • A hash chain alone cannot prove the ledger was never rewritten wholesale; checkpoints anchor it.
